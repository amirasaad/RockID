# Sprint 38 — Wave 1 Close + Next Distribution Decision

## Goal
Close Friends Beta Wave 1 with a clear product summary, retest asks, and the next distribution decision.

## Stories
| ID | Story | Acceptance Criteria | Priority |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `S38-1` | As the team, Wave 1 has a concise public summary for testers. | Summary issue includes top themes, fixed items, investigating items, and retest asks. | Must |
| `S38-2` | As the team, Wave 1 acceptance is explicitly evaluated. | 10+ reports, 24h triage, and high-priority resolution/defer criteria are marked pass/fail. | Must |
| `S38-3` | As the team, Wave 2 distribution is decided. | Decision records whether Wave 2 remains Android-only, waits for TestFlight, or uses another distribution path. | Must |
| `S38-4` | As the team, the next sprint starts from evidence. | Next sprint candidates are drawn from Wave 1 themes and Sprint 37 gate results. | Should |

## Planned Exit Criteria
- Friends Beta Wave 1 summary is published.
- Wave 1 acceptance criteria are complete or explicitly missed with follow-up.
- Next distribution path is documented.
- Kanban and Epic Milestones reflect Wave 1 outcome.

## Evidence To Capture
- Wave 1 summary issue.
- Acceptance checklist result.
- Distribution decision note.
- Retest asks for testers.

## Guardrails
- Do not expand to public launch from Wave 1 alone.
- Keep backend/cloud testing deferred unless explicitly re-scoped.
- Keep iOS TestFlight deferred until Apple Developer team access exists.
