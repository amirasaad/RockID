# Friends Beta Wave 1 — Acceptance Criteria

Window: May 8, 2026 to May 18, 2026

## Operational
- One test issue per template created and closed.
- At least one invited tester confirms:
  - wiki access
  - issue creation
  - screenshot upload
- Labels and milestone visible to collaborators.

## Product
- Offline capture/upload and analyze flow completes.
- Low-confidence messaging is understandable.
- Save/reopen flow is reliable.
- Wrong-result issues include enough detail for reproduction.

## Wave-Close Criteria
- At least 10 completed tester issue reports.
- 100% of reports triaged within 24h.
- All `beta-priority-high` issues resolved or explicitly deferred.
- Publish Wave 1 summary issue:
  - top themes
  - fixed items
  - retest asks
  - next sprint decisions
